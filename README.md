
# Employee Attrition Analysis

## Overview

This project analyses employee attrition using the IBM HR Analytics – Employee Attrition & Performance dataset.

The project was completed as part of the AnalystLab Africa Data Science Internship Programme, Week 1 assignment.

The purpose of the project is to understand the workforce, explore patterns associated with employee attrition, generate business-focused insights, and identify variables that may be useful for future predictive modelling.

---

## Business Problem

ABC Manufacturing Ltd wants to understand employee attrition before investing in predictive machine learning models.

The analysis focuses on understanding the workforce and identifying potential factors associated with employee attrition.

Understanding employee attrition can help organisations identify areas that may require further investigation and support evidence-based workforce and employee-retention decisions.

---

## Business Questions

The analysis investigates the following questions:

1. What does the company's workforce look like?
2. Which departments have the highest employee attrition?
3. Does age influence attrition?
4. Does monthly income affect retention?
5. Does overtime influence attrition?
6. Which job roles experience the highest turnover?
7. Which variables appear important for future predictive modelling?

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Understand the business problem

- Perform initial exploratory data analysis
- Create meaningful data visualisations
